Support

A question, a problem, a mistake in an exercise or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.

Senior Path Pro on a new device: Progrès › Senior Path Pro › Restaurer mes achats (restore purchases), with the same Apple Account.

Moving to a new phone: your progress is stored on the phone. It comes along when you set up the new phone from the old one's iCloud backup.

Starting over: Progrès › Effacer ma progression (erase my progress).
